
Runs of CLOCCS from March 2024. Both replicates were run with 1,000 simulated annealing and 100,000 burn-in and 100,000 sampling. These fits are consistent with shorter runs of 10,000 as well.

The notable fixes in CLOCCS were the fix to constrain gamma2 to be greater than gamma1. And to fix the alpha and tau values during simulated annealing at the beginning of burn-in. As well as the inclusion of halted cells in the model.

These fixes allow the model to converge to consistent parameter fits.
